The tokens

Every colour, radius and font in the kit is a custom property on :root, set again for dark. A page that wants its own look changes the tokens, and every component follows. Neutrals are warm greys, from the page's ground to the faintest text:

Besides colours: --radius (cards) and --radius-sm (fields, small boxes), --shadow and --shadow-pop (popups), --backdrop behind a modal, --font and --mono, --page-max (how wide .page grows), --side-w (the sidebar) and --topbar-h.

:root { --blue: #2563eb; --radius: 14px; }   /* the page's own blue and rounder cards */

Tones

A tone is a class that sets --c, the colour a component draws with. The same fourteen work on every component that has a colour: a pill, a bar, a stat's value, a button, a chart column, a progress bar, an avatar.

Status colours mean something: ok, warn and bad for good, needs a look and failed, idle for something switched off. The others are for telling things apart (a team, a kind). In a builder the tone is an argument: Dash.pill('Down', 'bad'), Dash.bar(0.7, 'warn').

Dash.tone(value, { 75: 'warn', 90: 'bad' }) picks a tone from thresholds: 'ok' below 75, 'warn' from 75, 'bad' from 90 ({ below: true } when lower is worse).

The lines of a line chart have four colours of their own. Every pair of them can be told apart in both themes, by colour-blind readers too, which isn't so of the tones. They are for lines, dots and keys, not for text.

A tinted block

.tint gives anything a soft fill and text in its tone: a highlighted note, a selected row of your own, a box around a warning.

Light and dark

A page follows the reader's system setting with nothing to do. To let them choose, give buttons data-theme-set with system, light or dark: the choice is saved, set as data-theme on the page, and the pressed button says so (aria-pressed). The docs' own switch, top right, is this:

  • Dash.theme.get() is the saved choice ('system', 'light' or 'dark'), Dash.theme.set(mode) changes it, Dash.theme.current() is what shows now.
  • Dash.color('blue') is a token's value at the moment, for drawing on a canvas in the right colours.
  • Your own CSS for dark goes under both @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root:not([data-theme="light"]) … } and :root[data-theme="dark"] …, the two ways the kit turns dark. The dark: utilities do both for you.

Forced colours and less motion

The system's high-contrast mode (forced colours) drops background colours and shadows, and the kit draws what they said another way. What shows data keeps its tones, inside a line in the system's ink: bars, meters, splits, columns, progress, legend keys, dots, lanes and the timeline. What is chosen or current takes the system's highlight: segments, chips, tabs, sidebar items, page numbers, the bottom nav, a switch that is on, and the primary button. Pills, tags, callouts, toasts and tooltips get an edge, a marked row or a ringed card an outline, and a text box a focus ring and a dashed border when it is wrong. The active suggestion and the chosen day in a calendar get an outline. A page's own pieces drawn with a background need the same: put forced-color-adjust: none on what shows data.

With reduced motion set, a running progress bar becomes a steady one and the kit's other motion stops.
