Lists and trees

Lists to read, to pick from and to put in order, trees, facts and timelines.

Lists

Dash.entries(rows, options) is a list of things, a line or two each: a lead (an avatar or an icon), a title and sub, and an end (a time, a pill). title and sub are keys or functions and are escaped; lead and end return HTML. href: (row) => url makes each a link, button: true a button (with key as its data-key, for a click handler and so a redraw keeps the focus). wrap: true lets long text wrap instead of ending in …; empty is the text when there are no rows.

By hand: ul.entries › li.entry (or an a.entry or button.entry in the li) › .entry-lead, .entry-text with .entry-title and .entry-sub, .entry-end.

Lists to pick from

Dash.list(el, options) writes the same rows and looks after them. It takes rows, title, sub, lead, end and href as Dash.entries does, and a key (id). With select: 'one' a click picks a row, with 'many' it ticks one; onSelect gets the row, or the rows. can(row) says which may be picked. menu(row) gives a row a menu (its ⋯ button, a right click or Shift+F10), and onMenu(value, row) gets what was chosen.

The arrow keys, Home, End and the first letters move between rows; Space picks. With 'many', Shift with a click or an arrow picks all between, and Ctrl+A (⌘A) all of them, then none. onPick(row, event) is a double click or Enter; without select it is a click. group is a field or a function whose values become headings. .update(rows) shows new rows and keeps what is picked, the focus and the scroll; .filter(text or test) shows those with the words, and returns how many. .selected, .select(keys), .rows, .shown, .count, .redraw() and .destroy() are there too.

Lists to put in order

reorder: true gives each row a grip. Drag it with a mouse or a finger (Escape calls it off), press the arrow keys on it, or click it for Move up, Move down and the ends. onMove(rows, { row, from, to }) gets the rows in the new order. Return a promise while it is saved: if it rejects, or gives false, the row is put back and a toast says why. An update that comes during a drag or a save waits until it is over; one from before the move doesn't undo it.

With groups, a row moves within its own. In a list to pick from, Alt with an arrow moves the row with the focus. .move(key, place) does it from code. A screen reader hears the new place: “Block bots: 2 of 4”.

Long lists

more: 20 shows twenty rows and a button for the next twenty. load(offset) gives the rows from there on, or a promise of them: the first part is asked for at once, each further one with the button, and the end is a part shorter than more. A part that fails says so, with Try again; .reload() starts over. height, a number of pixels, makes it a box that scrolls under its headings.

Trees

Dash.tree(el, options) shows things inside things. Each of items has an id and a title, and items of its own if it is a branch; key, title, sub and children name other fields, or are functions. lead(row, { branch, open }) and end(row) are HTML: an icon, a pill. open says what is open at the start: keys, a number of levels, or true for all; store: 'name' remembers what the reader opened.

A click on a branch opens or closes it; on a leaf it is onPick(row, event). The keys are those of a tree anywhere: Up and Down move, Right opens and goes in, Left closes and goes out, Home and End, * opens all the branches beside, and letters find a row. The focus stays on a row while the tree is redrawn. menu and onMenu work as on a list. .filter(text or test) shows the rows with the words, inside the branches they are in, and returns how many; an empty text shows all again, opened as before.

.update(items), .items, .expand(keys, on) (or true for all, false for none), .expanded, .reveal(key, { focus }) (opens what it is in and shows it), .redraw() and .destroy() are for code; onOpen(row, open) says what the reader opened and closed.

Trees to pick from

With select: 'one' a click picks a row, whether it is a branch or a leaf; the arrow in front of a branch, or a second click on it, opens and closes it. select: 'many' gives each row a box: ticking a branch ticks every leaf in it, also the ones that aren't shown, and a branch of which some is ticked has a dash. onSelect gets the row, or the ticked leaves. can(row) says which may be picked, selected what is at the start, and .select(keys) changes it.

Trees that load as they open

A row whose children are true has some that aren't there yet. load(row) gives them, or a promise of them, when it is first opened. While they are on their way the branch says so; if they can't be had it says why, with Try again. .reload(key) asks once more.

Boards

Dash.board(el, { columns, cards, column }) is cards in columns (a kanban board): column is the field of a card that says which one it is in. A card is moved with the mouse, anywhere on it, or with a finger by its grip; with the arrow keys on its grip; or from the menu its grip opens. onMove(cards, { card, from, to, index }) has the cards in their new order. Return a promise while it is saved: if it rejects, or gives false, the card is put back and a toast says why.

  • A column: { id, title, tone, limit, empty, add }. One with more cards than its limit is marked. onAdd(column) puts a + in each head (add: false leaves it out).
  • A card: title, sub, tone and key are fields or functions (escaped); lead(card), above the title, and end(card), below, are HTML; href makes it a link, onPick(card, event) a button. menu(card) and onMenu(value, card) are its own menu.
  • can(card, column) says which columns a card may go to; the others fade while it is dragged. move: false, or a function, is a board, or a card, that stays where it is.
  • height is how tall it gets; its columns then scroll. A column is at least --board-col wide (220 px); where they don't fit, the board scrolls sideways in itself, also while a card is held near its edge.

Moves show at once and are saved one after another; if one can't be, it goes back, and so does what was moved after it. An update that comes while a card is dragged or saved waits, and one from before the move doesn't undo it. .update(cards), .cards, .columns(list), .filter(text or test), .count, .move(key, column, index) (a promise of whether it stays there), .redraw(), .destroy().

By hand: .board › .board-col › .board-head (a .board-name, a .count-badge) and .board-zone › ul.board-cards › li.board-card, with a .board-open for its title, and .board-lead, .board-sub and .board-end. .board-add is the + in a head; a .board-card is .movable, and .toned with a tone (class="board-card toned warn"); a column is .no-drop while a card is dragged that can't go there.

Facts

.kv is a dl of names and values in columns (--cols, 2). .facts is a line of facts with dots between them, wrapping as it needs.

Timelines

Dash.timeline(items, { empty }) lists events on a line, newest first as given, each with a dot in its tone: { title, time, text, tone }. A time that Dash.time can read shows as "12 min ago" and keeps itself current; other text shows as it is.
