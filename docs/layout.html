Page layout

The top bar, the sidebar and its drawer, the page, grids, rows and the phone navigation.

The top barThe pageA sidebarThe sidebar as a drawerGrids, stacks and rowsOn a phone

The top bar

.topbar stays at the top while the page scrolls. It holds a .brand (a .mark and the name in an h1 or .brand-name), an optional .sub line, and .top-right, which pushes what's in it to the right. It wraps onto more lines on a phone, and scrolls away there instead of staying.

  • .topstat is a reading: a name in b, an optional .meter, and the value in .v. A tone on the .topstat colours the meter and the value.
  • .top-link is a quiet link or button. .side-toggle is the square button that folds the sidebar (give it data-toggle-side).
  • .topbar.static-md lets the bar scroll away from 900 px down too, for a page whose sidebar stacks above the content there.

The page

.page holds the content, centred, up to --page-max wide (1320 px; .page.wide 1480, .page.narrow 1000). A .page-head with a .page-title and a .page-note opens it; each .section has a .section-title, or a .section-head when something sits beside the title. .kicker is a small bold muted label, and .footer small print at the end.

A sidebar

.shell puts a .sidebar beside the .content. The sidebar holds .side-head labels and .nav-item links or buttons. An item has an optional .nav-icon, a .nav-main with a .nav-name and a .nav-sub, and a .nav-meta on the right. aria-current="page" marks where the reader is; .attn with a tone tints one that needs a look.

A button with data-toggle-side folds the sidebar to a 64 px rail of icons (.shell.collapsed) and back; the choice is remembered (Dash.config.sideKey), and document gets a dash:side event with e.detail.collapsed. --side-w sets the sidebar's width.

The sidebar as a drawer

Add .offcanvas to the shell and, from 900 px down, the sidebar becomes a drawer over the page. The same data-toggle-side button opens it there (and still folds the rail on a wider screen). While it's open, the rest of the page is inert; Escape, a click outside it or a link inside closes it, and the focus goes back to the button. These docs use it: make the window narrow and press ≡.

Dash.side.open() and Dash.side.close() do it from code; open returns false on a wider screen, where there is no drawer.

Grids, stacks and rows

  • .grid: equal columns, two unless --cols says more, one from 900 px down. .span-2 spans two.
  • .cards: as many cards across as fit, each at least --card-min (300 px) wide.
  • .vstack: a column with a 14 px gap. .row: a line that wraps, with gaps. .toolbar: a row for a search box and filters above a table.
  • .push pushes itself, and what follows, to the right of a row; .grow takes the room that's left.

For anything else, the layout utilities give flex and grid with widths: grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-4.

On a phone

.bottom-nav is a row of links along the bottom of a phone (640 px down; hidden wider). Each is an a with an svg and a word, and aria-current="page" on the one you're on. It has to be a direct child of <body>: the page then gets room under it. .fab is a round floating button for the page's main action (btn primary fab; .fab.extended has a label). It sits above the bottom navigation.

.hide-sm hides something on a phone, .show-sm shows it only there.
