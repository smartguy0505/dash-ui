Panels

.panel is a section of a page: a card-coloured box with a border. Its .panel-head holds the title (an h2 or h3) and, on the right, a .read (a reading or count), a .seg or a .btn. .panel-note is small print at its foot. .panel.flush has no padding, for a table or a picture that runs to its edges.

Cards

.card is one of many, in .cards (as many across as fit, each at least --card-min, 300 px). Inside: a .card-head with a .card-title (one line) and things beside it, a .card-sub, and a .card-foot that sits at the bottom, so cards in a row line up. .card-media is a picture across the top.

Stat tiles

.stat is a number at a glance: a .label, a .value (with a small unit) and a one-line .note (.note.wrap may take two). .stats lays them out, as many as fit at --stat-min (180 px) and two across on a phone; .stats.fixed puts exactly --stat-cols (4) across. Dash.stat(label, value, { unit, note, tone, title }) writes one, the value in its tone. A button.stat can be clicked.

The big number

.hero is the one number a page is about, with what explains it beside it (it stacks on a phone). .big is the number, with a small unit; .hero-line a line under it, with b for figures.

Status banners

.banner is the page's status in one line with a big dot: a .badge (with an i inside), a .banner-title and a .banner-text. .banner.active tints it and pulses the dot, so banner active bad is an incident going on now.

Callouts and rings

.callout is a soft block for a note, tinted when it has a tone, with a .callout-title. .callout.solid is coloured text on its own. .ring on a panel or card rings it in orange (or its tone), for the one that needs attention.

Lists

Dash.entries(rows, options) is a list of things, a line or two each: a lead (an avatar or an icon), a title and sub, and an end (a time, a pill). title and sub are keys or functions and are escaped; lead and end return HTML. href: (row) => url makes each a link, button: true a button (with key as its data-key, for a click handler and so a redraw keeps the focus). wrap: true lets long text wrap instead of ending in …; empty is the text when there are no rows.

By hand: ul.entries › li.entry (or an a.entry or button.entry in the li) › .entry-lead, .entry-text with .entry-title and .entry-sub, .entry-end.

Accordions

.accordion joins <details> blocks into one box, with a chevron on each summary. Give them the same name and the browser keeps one open at a time. A lone <details> anywhere is styled too.

Why is a server grey?

It hasn't reported for five minutes. It turns red after fifteen.

Can I move a server to another team?

Yes: open it and choose Move. Its history moves with it.

How long is history kept?

Thirteen months, so a year can be compared with the one before.

Empty and loading

.empty is what a list shows with nothing in it: an .empty-title (or h2), a line, and a button. .empty.boxed is a dashed box, for a whole page with nothing yet. .skel is a loading line (.wide, .mid, .short), until the data comes.
