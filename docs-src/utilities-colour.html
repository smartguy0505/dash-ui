Text colour

Every token is a colour class: text-muted, text-bad, text-purple. text-tone is the current tone (--c), so text-tone warn is in the warning colour. They follow light and dark by themselves.

muted · faint · blue · ok · warn · bad · tone

Background

The same tokens as backgrounds, and tints: bg-blue/10 is the blue at 10 % over what's behind it (5, 10, 15, 20, 30 and 50). A tint reads well in both themes, where a solid colour usually needs text-card on top.

bg-blue/10bg-ok/15 bg-bad/20bg-purple text-cardbg-soft

Border colour

Border colours from the tokens, with tints /20, /30 and /50. They take hover:, focus:, focus-visible: and focus-within:.
